Who We Are

TrashAway is Nigeria's leading on-demand waste management platform

Founded in 2023, TrashAway began with a simple observation: Nigeria needed a better way to manage waste. Traditional methods were inefficient, unreliable, and harmful to our environment.

We built TrashAway to change that. Our platform connects households and businesses with verified waste collectors, making pickup convenient, transparent, and eco-friendly.

Today, we've completed over 50,000 pickups, served 10,000+ homes, and recycled 80% of collected waste. We're just getting started.

Our Core Values

Sustainability First

Transparency & Trust

Our Journey

2023

Founded TrashAway

Our founding team recognized Nigeria's waste crisis and decided to build a solution. We started in Ibadan with 10 collectors.

2024

Expanded to 5 Cities

High demand led us to launch in Lagos, Kano, Port Harcourt, and Abuja. We completed 25,000 pickups and recycled 10,000 tons of waste.

2026

50,000+ Pickups

We've grown into Nigeria's most trusted waste management platform with 10,000+ active users and 500+ verified collectors across the nation.
