Help & Support

Find answers to common questions. We're here to help!

Browse Topics

How do I sign up on TrashAway?
Download the TrashAway app or visit our website, click "Sign Up", enter your email and phone number, verify your phone, set your address, and you're ready! The whole process takes less than 2 minutes.
Is TrashAway available in my area?
TrashAway is currently available in Lagos, Ibadan, Kano, Port Harcourt, and Abuja. We're expanding to more cities soon. Check your area on our app to see service availability.
What waste types do you accept?
We accept household waste, recyclables (plastic, paper, glass, metal), organic waste, and commercial waste. Some hazardous materials are restricted. Check our app for a complete list of accepted waste types.
Do you provide waste bins?
We don't provide permanent bins, but you can use your own bags or containers. For large-scale commercial pickups, contact our sales team for specialized solutions.
How much does TrashAway cost?
We offer flexible plans: Free (Basic), ₦2,500/month (Pro), and ₦8,500/month (Business). Each plan includes different features and benefits. See our Pricing page for details.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and mobile money (MTN, Airtel, 9Mobile). All payments are secure and encrypted with SSL technology.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes! There are no contracts or hidden fees. Cancel anytime from your account settings. Your cancellation takes effect immediately, and no refunds are issued for partial months.
How do I update my payment method?
Go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods. You can add, edit, or remove payment methods anytime. Changes take effect on your next billing date.
How quickly can I get a pickup?
Basic plan users get standard pickup within 4 hours. Pro members get priority pickup within 60 minutes. Business customers get express pickup within 30 minutes. Request a pickup anytime, 24/7.
Can I reschedule or cancel a pickup?
Yes! You can reschedule or cancel pickups through your app up to 30 minutes before the scheduled time. No cancellation fees apply.
What if my collector doesn't show up?
If your collector doesn't arrive within 15 minutes of the scheduled time, contact support immediately. We'll send a replacement collector or refund your service fee.
Can I schedule recurring pickups?
Yes! Pro and Business plans include automatic scheduling. Set pickups for daily, weekly, or custom intervals, and we'll collect on schedule automatically.
How do Eco Points work?
Every pickup earns Eco Points based on waste sorted and recycled. Pro members earn 2x points. Basic members earn 1x points per pickup. You'll see points in your app immediately after pickup completion.
How do I redeem Eco Points?
Go to the Rewards section in your app to redeem points for discounts, cash back, or donate to environmental projects. 100 points = ₦100 in value.
Do Eco Points expire?
No! Your Eco Points never expire. Accumulate them over time and redeem whenever you want. There's no time limit on rewards redemption.
Can I transfer Eco Points to others?
Eco Points are tied to your account and cannot be transferred. However, you can redeem them for gift cards and share those with friends and family.
The app keeps crashing. What should I do?
Try uninstalling and reinstalling the app. Make sure you have the latest version from your app store. Clear your cache from phone settings. If the issue persists, contact support@trashaway.com.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. Enter your email, and we'll send a password reset link. Click the link to set a new password.
The app won't accept my phone number. Why?
Make sure you've entered a valid Nigerian phone number starting with +234 or 0. If the issue continues, try using a different phone or contact support.
I'm having trouble with GPS tracking. What can I do?
Ensure location services are enabled on your phone. Go to Settings > Privacy > Location Services and allow TrashAway access. Restart the app if tracking isn't working.
How do I become a TrashAway Collector?
Download our Collector app, register with your details, pass our background check, and complete onboarding training. You'll need a valid ID, phone number, and transport option.
How much can I earn as a collector?
Earnings vary based on pickups completed and customer ratings. Collectors typically earn ₦3,000-₦8,000 per day. Top collectors earn ₦12,000+ daily. Payments are made weekly.
Do you provide uniforms and equipment?
Yes! TrashAway provides uniforms, gloves, and safety equipment at no cost. You're responsible for providing your own transport (motorcycle, van, or truck).
What if I need to take time off?
You can set your availability in the Collector app. Mark yourself unavailable for specific dates/times, and you won't receive job requests. Flexibility is built into our system!
