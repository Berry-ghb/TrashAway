Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes! There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings. Your cancellation takes effect immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and mobile money options (MTN, Airtel, 9Mobile). All transactions are secure and encrypted.

How do Eco Points work?

Every pickup earns Eco Points based on the waste sorted and recycled. Pro plan users earn 2x points. You can redeem points for discounts, cash back, or donate to environmental projects.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes! Pay annually and save 15%. You can switch to annual billing from your account settings anytime.
