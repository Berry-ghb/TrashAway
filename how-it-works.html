Our Process In Detail

Download & Sign Up

Create your TrashAway account in seconds. Verify your phone number and set your address. You're ready to request pickups anytime!

Organize Your Waste

Sort your waste into bags or containers. Select the waste type (household, recyclables, commercial). Set a pickup time that suits you best.

We Pick It Up

Your agent arrives within 60 minutes, collects your waste, and loads it into our truck. You'll receive a pickup confirmation and receipt instantly.

We Recycle & Dispose

80% of collected waste is recycled. The remainder is responsibly disposed of at certified facilities. You earned Eco Points for supporting a greener Nigeria!
